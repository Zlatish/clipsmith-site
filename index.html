ClipSmith

A personal tool that turns gaming stream VODs into short vertical clips.

ClipSmith runs on its owner's own computer. It finds highlights in stream recordings, makes short clips with captions, and uploads them to the owner's own YouTube and TikTok channels. Every clip credits the original streamer.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

  • Who uses it: ClipSmith is used only by its owner, to manage the owner's own channels. It has no other users and no public sign-up.
  • Google / YouTube data: when the owner connects YouTube, ClipSmith uses the YouTube Data API to upload the owner's clips to the owner's channel and to read basic channel details and view counts for those uploads. Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer.
  • What is not done: ClipSmith does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties, does not use Google user data for advertising, and does not collect data about anyone else.
  • Removing access: access can be removed at any time from Google account permissions or with the Disconnect button in ClipSmith, which deletes the stored tokens.
  • ClipSmith's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube uploads are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
